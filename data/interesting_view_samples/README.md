# Interesting camera view samples

Ten user-selected FAA JPEG snapshots from September 28, 2026 are labeled `interesting_good`. `manifest.json` records each camera, exact FAA image URL, capture time, and selection time. Open `gallery.html` to review the saved frames.

These examples represent visually interesting cloud or weather scenes, whether or not a storm is present. They are separate from the storm-presence and view-quality labels in `data/camera_view_samples`. The current OpenCLIP storm and sunset filters do not use this collection; a future general interesting-view scorer will need negative examples and independent validation before these images become training references.

# Sunset camera view samples

The twenty-six JPEGs in this directory are fixed copies of seventeen good and nine bad camera views selected for the sunset filter. The eighth good view is the McClure Mountain sunset, whose experimental GFS forecast score was only 12/100. The ninth is the McCullough Range sunset; the next six are Zephyr, Sierra at Tahoe, NOAA Reno, Hillside, Big Hill, and Yosemite Lakes. The latest two good views are FAA frames from Queen Bee looking south and Mammoth Mountain looking north. The latest bad views are from Two Rivers and an FAA camera. `manifest.json` records each original link and the image URL downloaded for it. FAA camera-page links were resolved to their latest frame when the sample was collected; USGS `_newest.jpg` links were copied at collection time. Keeping the JPEGs here prevents later live-frame changes from silently changing the reference set.

The sunset scorer uses these seventeen good images as positive references. Its negative references are the nine bad images here plus all thirteen negative references from `data/camera_view_samples/manifest.json`. It adds a camera's warm-color strength across the entire frame to its OpenCLIP good-minus-rejected similarity margin. The combined cutoff is calibrated on leave-one-out scores from these examples. Labels are user judgments about useful sunset views, not verified meteorological observations.
